Why capital from the beginning

A life is long. Money given early — even a modest amount — can sit and compound while she learns to walk, to read, to choose a path. This page exists so people who love her can contribute to a dedicated pot, without turning a birth into a catalogue or a payment form.

She is not named here on purpose. The branding stays generic: gift capital for a little one. Parents hold any future account as custodians. Your allocation is a wish they may follow — not an order, and not a way to buy a sleeve or any security through this site.

How a gift works

Step 1

Choose a mix

Pick a preset or set your own percentages across four sleeves. They must add up to 100%.

Step 2

Save the preference code

The site writes your wish into a short code. Nothing is sent to a server — it stays in this browser, and later it can go in a bank reference.

Step 3

Wait for the account

Do not transfer yet. Konto folgt. When the Juniorkonto is published, you can send a SEPA transfer from your own bank. This website never processes a payment.

    Keep this code. When the account is published, paste it as the transfer reference so parents can match your wish. It is not a trade ticket.

    The preference code appears when the four sleeves add up to 100%.

    Format: GC1-GGG-BBB-SSS-PPP-TTTT — growth, balanced, steady, parents (percentages), then a short token. Example: GC1-070-020-010-000-K7MM means 70% growth, 20% balanced, 10% steady. Parents can decode it in the ledger helper.
