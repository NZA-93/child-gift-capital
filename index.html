Why capital from the beginning

A life is long. Money given early — even a modest amount — can sit and compound while a little one learns to walk, to read, to choose a path. This page exists so people who love that child can contribute to a dedicated pot, without turning a birth into a catalogue or a payment form.

The child is not named here on purpose. The branding stays generic: gift capital for a little one. The account is held by parents who are German tax residents, as custodians. Your allocation is a wish they can see and honour — not an order, and not a purchase of securities through this site.

How a gift works

Step 1

Choose a mix

Pick a preset or set your own percentages across four sleeves. They must add up to 100%.

Step 2

Copy the reference

The site writes your wish into a short transfer code. Nothing is sent to a server — it stays in your browser and in the bank reference.

Step 3

Transfer from your bank

Use your own bank’s SEPA transfer to the dedicated IBAN. This website never processes a payment.

The four sleeves

Parents execute investments later. These labels are a shared language for your preference.

Equity and bonds together

Balanced

Calmer path

Steady

Bonds and cash-oriented holdings. Less drama, less long-run growth in ordinary years.

How to give

Open your own bank app. Send a SEPA credit transfer. Paste the preference code as the reference (Verwendungszweck) so parents can match the gift in their ledger.
